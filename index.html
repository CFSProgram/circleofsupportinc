About Circle of Support Inc.

We are a culturally responsive and family-centered agency supporting adults with developmental disabilities in living fuller, more independent lives within their family homes.

Circle of Support Inc. is dedicated to supporting individuals with developmental disabilities in improving their quality of life through community building and independent growth. Our Coordinated Family Support program is designed to support adults who choose to live with their families and benefit from access to regional center and community resources.

  • ✓ Regional center consumers
  • ✓ Family-home based services
  • ✓ Community-integrated support
  • ✓ Culturally responsive care
  • ✓ Training for consumers and families

What We Do

Our program is personalized to each consumer and family, focusing on skills, independence, training, scheduling, backup support, future planning, and access to meaningful community resources.

📅

Scheduling of Services

We help coordinate appointments, community activities, and service routines while keeping a clear and reliable schedule for the family.

🎓

Training & Education

We provide tailored support in communication, self-care, health literacy, transportation, social skills, and access to regional and community services.

🛟

Backup Support

We help families create contingency plans for unexpected changes in routine, transportation, appointments, or caregiver transitions.

🚀

Maximize Independence

We focus on skill-building in self-care, household tasks, travel, money management, communication, and community participation.

📚

Additional Resources

We connect families to useful information and resources related to diagnoses, Medi-Cal, SSI, Medicare, CalFresh, and community support programs.

🏡

Future Planning

We support long-term planning for family transitions, housing, aging caregivers, supported living options, and maintaining safety and dignity.

Our program is centered on the individual, while also recognizing the essential role of the consumer's circle of support. We believe in culturally responsive care, family partnership, and practical training that respect each person's goals and lived experience.
